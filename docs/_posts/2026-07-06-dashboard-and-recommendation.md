The **[interactive dashboard]({{ site.baseurl }}/dashboard/)** is the heart of
the project: search any of the 91 counties, color the map by 32 different
measures (from smoking prevalence to drive time to the nearest stroke center),
and read a plain-language summary of what drives each county's scores.

The **Risk vs. Access matrix** plots every county by stroke risk against access
to care — the top-right quadrant is where intervention matters most.

**Coming next: per-county recommendations.** Each county page will explain
*why* it scores the way it does (its top risk drivers) and what kinds of
initiatives fit — mobile screening events, telestroke partnerships, prevention
programs — generated from the data itself.

Discord 7/7).*
